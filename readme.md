## Summary of Findings

### Some Notable Examples of Significant Outcomes (Positive Signal)
Thirty five (of forty seven) projects yielded results that warrant further investigation.

*   **Geophysical Correlations**:
    *   **Project 45 (Seismicity)** identified a robust inverse correlation between lunar phase and earthquake frequency, particularly at the Quarters ( p< 0.001).
    *   **Project 47 (Sleep)** confirmed a strong physiological response to the Lunar Tithi cycle, with Deep Sleep dropping by ~16 minutes at the Full Moon ( p< 0.00001).
    *   **Project 02 (Economic Cycles)** and **Project 39 (Retrograde Markets)** found significant periodicities in financial data aligning with planetary cycles.

*   **Psychological & Behavioral**:
    *   **Project 29 (Asteroids)** and **Project 07 (ML Personality)** successfully extracted thematic clusters from birth charts that align with psychological profiles ( p< 0.001).
    *   **Project 13 (Personality)** used circular statistics to find angular distributions of planets in the charts of specific professionals that deviated significantly from uniformity.

*   **Methodological Success**:
    *   **Project 44 (Rectification)** demonstrated that Machine Learning can reverse-engineer birth times with a Mean Absolute Error of 2.71 hours, validating the internal consistency of transit logic.

### Some Notable Examples of Null Findings (No Effect)
A number of rigorous studies failed to reject the null hypothesis, suggesting that certain traditional techniques may be apophenic or require more nuance:

*   **Relationship Astrology**:
    *   **Project 36 (Synastry)** found no significant logistic correlation between traditional synastry aspects and the longevity of relationships/marriage.
    *   **Project 38 (Group Dynamics)** showed no composite chart effect on band longevity (p=0.52).

*   **Short-Term Perception**:
    *   **Project 05 (Retrogrades)** found no objective increase in flight delays during Mercury Retrograde (p=0.21), suggesting the phenomenon is largely perceptual bias.
    *   **Project 37 (Mood Surveys)** found no correlation between daily planetary transits and self-reported mood (p=0.90).

*   **Medical Astrology**:
    *   **Project 40 (Decumbiture)** failed to predict illness duration or severity using traditional chart casting methods (p=0.98).

### Conclusion
The research suggests a divide: **Geophysical and biological variables** (Sleep, Earthquakes, broad Economic trends) show stronger, more consistent correlations with celestial mechanics than **specific individual psychological or event-based predictions** (Synastry, Daily Mood, Flight Delays). Future research should prioritize mechanism-agnostic signal detection in large physical datasets over small-scale psychological studies.
