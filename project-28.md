# Project 28: Solar Houses and Life Domains

> **Book:** *The Big Astrology Book of Research* by Renay Oshop
> **Source:** [bigastrologybook.com/2/research](https://bigastrologybook.com/2/research)

---

## 🌟 Overview — What We Asked

Does the distribution of planets *relative to the Sun* — measured as Solar Houses — predict career domain or life focus across creative, political, scientific, entertainment, and athletic populations?

---

## 💡 Why This Matters

Traditional house research is stuck on two problems: houses require precise birth times, and there are 20+ competing house systems with no consensus. Solar Houses solve both: treat the Sun as the Ascendant, measure every other planet's angular distance from the Sun, and assign them to 12 houses of 30° each. Result: a chart from a birthdate alone, independent of birth time and house system debates.

Project 18 validated the Solar House methodology statistically (BF = 23.5). This project applies it to a wider, more categorically diverse celebrity sample to look for career archetypes.

Mercury and Venus have elongation constraints that cluster them near the Sun, biasing the 1st and 2nd Solar Houses slightly upward for these bodies specifically.*

## 📈 Results

### Solar House Emphasis by Career Category

*Percentage of planets in each Solar House. Uniform baseline = 8.33%.*

| Solar House | Politics | Entertainment | Arts | Science | Sports | Domain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1st (0–30°)** | **22.2%** | 11.1% | 10.2% | 11.7% | 11.4% | Solar Identity |
| **7th (180–210°)** | 5.6% | 7.8% | **16.7%** | 8.9% | 10.5% | The Other / Audience |
| **9th (240–270°)** | 13.0% | **18.5%** | 8.2% | 8.3% | 8.6% | Global reach |
| **10th (270–300°)** | 5.6% | **18.5%** | 6.1% | 7.8% | 11.4% | Fame / Career apex |
| **5th (120–150°)** | 3.7% | 11.1% | 9.2% | **13.1%** | 8.6% | Creativity / Play |
| **12th (330–360°)** | 5.6% | 2.2% | **16.7%** | 9.4% | 5.7% | Subconscious / Hidden |

**Politicians: 1st Solar House at 22.2%** — a **+166% deviation** from the 8.33% baseline. More than a fifth of all planetary placements for politicians fall within 30° of the Sun, essentially conjunct the solar identity. Leadership requires projecting strong personal presence — and this dataset's political figures cluster their charts around the solar self more than any other group.

**Entertainers: Twin peaks at 9th and 10th Solar Houses (18.5% each)** — +122% above baseline for both. Global reach and public reputation at the apex of one's field: exactly the twin requirements of entertainment stardom.

**Artists: 7th and 12th Solar Houses (16.7% each)** — the Audience house and the Subconscious house at +100% each. Artists create *for* someone (7th House: the Other) by channeling inner depths (12th House: the Hidden). Both Jupiter-ruled and hidden/other themes dominate.

**Scientists: Unexpected peak at 5th Solar House (13.1%)** — the house of Creativity, Play, and Speculation. Scientific discovery is partly a creative act — generating novel hypotheses, testing speculations, playing at the frontier of the unknown. This is the project's most counterintuitive single finding.

### Elemental Emphasis by Career Category

| Category | Fire % | Earth % | Air % | Water % | Dominant |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Politics** | **31.5%** | 22.2% | 27.8% | 18.5% | Fire |
| Entertainment | 25.6% | 25.6% | **29.6%** | **29.6%** | Air/Water tied |
| Arts | 22.4% | 22.4% | 19.4% | **35.7%** | Water |
| Science | 23.9% | 26.1% | 25.0% | 25.0% | Uniform |
| Sports | 25.7% | **31.4%** | 22.9% | 20.0% | Earth |

**Politics leads in Fire** (31.5% vs. 25% baseline) — the element of initiative, will, and assertion. **Sports leads in Earth** (31.4%) and has the *lowest Water* of any category (20.0%) — consistent with the idea that emotional groundedness and physical focus, rather than emotional sensitivity, drive peak athletic performance. **Arts leads in Water** (35.7%) — emotional depth and sensitivity. **Science is nearly uniform** across all four elements.

---

## 🔍 What the Numbers Mean

The archetypes that emerge are coherent and interpretively satisfying:

- Politicians project solar identity (1st House, Fire) — they *are* the brand
- Entertainers need global reach and reputation (9th and 10th Houses) — they need to be seen everywhere and remembered at the apex
- Artists project outward (7th House, audience) from inner depths (12th House, subconscious) — creation as externalization of the interior
- Scientists play (5th House) — the childlike curiosity and speculative creativity that discovery requires
- Athletes ground (Earth) and reduce emotional noise (lowest Water) — the body over the feeling

---

## ⚠️ Limitations & Caveats

**Small sample sizes:** Politics (N=6), Entertainment (N=3), Arts (N=8). Planet counts per category are reasonable for descriptive analysis but insufficient for formal chi-square testing (requires ≥5 expected counts per cell).

**Multiple comparisons:** 5 categories × 12 houses × 4 elements = 140 potential comparisons. Several deviations expected by chance. No correction applied to heatmap analysis — findings are descriptive, not confirmed.

**Mercury/Venus elongation constraint:** These bodies cannot appear far from the Sun in any natal chart. Their Solar House distributions would not be uniform even randomly. This biases the 1st and 2nd Solar Houses slightly for all categories.

---

## 🌟 Conclusion

Solar Houses produce archetypally coherent patterns across career categories without birth time data:

- **Politicians** concentrate planets near the Sun (1st Solar House, +166%)
- **Entertainers** cluster at fame-related houses (9th and 10th, +122%)
- **Artists** emphasize the Audience (7th) and Subconscious (12th) houses
- **Scientists** unexpectedly peak in the Creativity house (5th)
- **Athletes** show the lowest Water content of any category

The methodology's practical advantage is access: any birthday produces Solar House positions, enabling research into populations without verified birth times. Project 18's Bayes Factor validation supports the framework. Next step: scale to N≥100 per category to enable formal statistical testing.
