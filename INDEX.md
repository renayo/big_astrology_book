## 🗺️ Cross-Project Themes

### The Hardship Hypothesis
Five separate projects converge on one counterintuitive finding: **exceptional achievers tend to have "difficult" charts, not easy ones.**

- **Project 06:** Harmonic tension (H4 Squares, H7 Septiles) is elevated in high achievers; ease (H3 Trines) is not
- **Project 14:** Scientists have *debilitated* Tropical charts on average (mean −3.15)
- **Project 20:** The two strongest celebrity placements are Mars in Detriment and Moon in Fall
- **Project 33:** Writers cluster in weak-Mars charts; Filmmakers in strong-Sun
- **Project 01:** Amavasya (dark Moon phase) is most common in the billionaire cohort

Traditional astrology says strong, dignified placements describe capable, successful people. The data consistently says the opposite.

### Scale Kills Folk Mythology
The largest datasets produce the most definitive nulls — and they target the most culturally durable claims:

- **Project 03** (156M births): Full Moon has zero influence on birth timing. Ratio: 1.001.
- **Project 05** (24 years data): Mercury retrograde anomaly vanishes after detrending. Ratio: 1.002.
- **Project 26** (2,722 couples): Compatibility score predicts relationship duration at r=0.009.

### The Strongest Positive Signal Is About Clustering, Not Signs
**Project 17** tested 498 historical events and found mean planetary clustering (Sum of Cosines) is +148% higher on event days than random days (p<0.0001). The metric doesn't ask which sign Saturn is in — it asks how bunched the planets are overall. This aggregate measure outperformed every traditional named-aspect approach.

### Demographics Predict Belief Better Than Astrology Predicts Anything
**Project 04** found that urbanization, education, and income explain 74.3% of variation in state-level astrological search interest. That R² is higher than anything astrology achieves for any outcome in this book.

### The Zodiac Systems May Capture Different Things
Rather than one zodiac being "correct," the evidence across Projects 08, 14, 23, and 33 suggests:
- **Tropical zodiac** encodes seasonal rhythms — relevant where biology and social calendars matter
- **Sidereal zodiac** encodes constellational/stellar reference — shows stronger clustering signals in some contexts
