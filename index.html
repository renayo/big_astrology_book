Patterns Across the Research

Cross-project themes

⚔️ The Hardship Hypothesis

Five projects converge: exceptional achievers tend to have difficult charts, not easy ones. Tension in the birth chart may correlate with drive.

  • Project 06: Harmonic tension elevated in high achievers
  • Project 14: Scientists have debilitated Tropical charts
  • Project 20: Mars in Detriment & Moon in Fall dominate
  • Project 01: Amavasya (dark Moon) most common in billionaires

📏 Scale Kills Folk Mythology

The largest datasets produce the most definitive nulls — targeting the most culturally durable claims.

  • Project 03 (156M births): Full Moon → zero influence
  • Project 05 (24 years): Mercury Rx → calendar confound
  • Project 26 (2,722 couples): Compatibility → r=0.009

🌌 Clustering Beats Signs

Project 17: mean planetary clustering is +148% higher on historical event days vs random days (p<0.0001). Diffuse geometry outperforms every named aspect.

🗺️ Demographics > Astrology

Project 04: urbanization, education, and income explain 74.3% of variation in astrological belief — higher than astrology achieves for any outcome in this book.

☯️ Two Zodiacs, Two Questions

Projects 08, 14, 23, 33 suggest Tropical encodes seasonal rhythms while Sidereal shows stronger clustering in some contexts. Neither is simply "correct."
